# TapCatForever 1.5.3.4

**Professions**
- The later professions now need much more Trade Gold earned in total: Herbalism 1B, First Aid 50T, Fishing 1Qa and Cooking 50Qa (before: 25M, 2.5B, 250B and 2T). Trade Gold grows fast, so the old amounts came far too soon. Now you need a few rounds of selling your business for Renown, which makes your gold come faster, to reach them.
- Professions you already learned stay learned.
- Renown alone no longer unlocks a profession; only Trade Gold earned does.

**Chests**
- A chest you open while the reel is still spinning now gets its own spin right after, instead of skipping it.
