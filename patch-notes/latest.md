# TapCatForever 1.7.5

**Companion cats you can really see**
- Some companions showed an empty box. They were cats from a newer WoW that isn't in WoW: Forever. Every companion is now a cat that's really in your game, and there are 62 of them.
- New ones include WoW: Forever's own Skyborne cats, armored war sabers with glowing eyes, druid prowlers, wild frostsabers and a Diamond Tiger. The Flamecat and the Spectral Tiger (now the Armored Spectral Tiger) finally show up too.
- The dungeon and raid cats have new looks too, from the Alley Cat in the Deadmines to Mr. Bigglesworth in Naxxramas.
- If you bought a companion that's no longer in the list, you got your TapCoins back. The White Kitten is one of them: it looked just like Mr. Bigglesworth.

**Dungeon cats come from clearing the dungeon**
- A dungeon's companion joins you when you clear that dungeon or raid in Adventure, beating its last boss. Clearing it again counts too.
- Progress from before no longer hands them out. Every dungeon cat you had goes back for now, so clear its dungeon again to get it back.
- Every other companion is bought with TapCoins, and they cost what they did.

**Companion Stroll**
- It costs 500,000 TapCoins now, half what it was.
- Your companion stays close to your cat instead of wandering off, stands level with it, and is a little bigger.
- Its tail and legs aren't cut off by a box any more, even when your cat is big.

**Party cats side**
- A new option in Settings, next to Show party cats, puts the party cats on the right or left of your cat. Your companion walks on the other side.
