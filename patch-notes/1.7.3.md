# TapCatForever 1.7.3

- The "Your spot" tag over your newest gathering spot is gone. The gold frame on that row stays.
- Tooltips and the recipe list no longer say how likely a skill point is ("always", "often" and so on), since the chance changed and the words were wrong. The colours still show it, and you see your skill go up in the bar.

**Finding things**
- A new Materials page in the Compendium lists every material in the game with where it comes from (which gathering spot, rare find, daily craft, garden, fishing or cooking) and what it's used in. It has a search box.
- Recipe tooltips say where to get any material you're short on, and so does the Profession Bag.
- The Item Sets page now starts with the cosmetic sets: the 11 legendary crafted sets (with their profession) and the dungeon sets. Click one to see its pieces and try them on.
- The recipe list has a Sets button that shows only set recipes, so the legendary sets are easy to find.
