# TapCatForever 1.5.3.2

**Realm leaderboard**
- The leaderboard now shares through your party, raid and guild, the same way party cats work. Group up or be in a guild with friends who joined and you see each other right away, and they pass on the players they know, so the board grows as you play together.
- When someone joins your group, your cat is shared with them within a few seconds.
- WoW: Forever doesn't seem to allow addon messages on custom channels, which the leaderboard relied on until now, so players often didn't show up. It still tries that channel where the game allows it.
