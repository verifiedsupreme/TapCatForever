# TapCatForever 1.7.7
5 new Legendaries named for the TapCat crew, each with its own look and effects on your cat: a druid harness, a bow, a hammer, priest robes and a wand.

**5 new Legendaries**
- Five one of a kind Legendaries, made for TapCatForever and named for the TapCat crew:
  - N8 Dawg's Alpha Pack Harness, a leather chest for Druids
  - Caffeinated's Double Espresso, a bow for Hunters
  - Battledancer's Hammer of Justice, a two-handed hammer for Paladins
  - Shua's Vestments of the Dawn, robes for Priests
  - Ziggu's Wand of Endless Taps, a wand for Mages, Priests and Warlocks
- They're level 60 and found like every Legendary: in a Heroic Cache or a Raider's Hoard opened by a level 60 cat. The chest picks one your cat's class can use.
- Each has its own look on your cat and its own icon, nothing like any other item.
- And they look the part. Each one shines and has its own effects:
  - glowing paw prints and swirling leaves off the harness
  - coffee beans and curls of steam off the bow
  - golden music notes and a turning holy rune above the hammer
  - a sun halo blazing behind the robes, with feathers drifting up
  - tiny glowing cat heads and tap stars spilling off the wand
- Ziggu's wand shows in your cat's paw all the time, not only in fights.
- Legendary tooltips now say where Legendaries come from instead of "World drop".
