# TapCatForever 1.6.3

**Try things on**
- Shift-click an item to try it on your cat: gear on the Gear page, cosmetics and skins on the Appearance page, the Shop's offers and your racial accessory, crafting recipes, and anything in the Compendium (it opens the main window). It stays on your cat while you look around, so you can put a hat and a cape together. Shift-click it again to take it off, or press Clear preview under your cat. Nothing is equipped or bought.
- The tooltips say "Shift-click to try it on your cat." Closing the window or switching class takes the previewed items off.
