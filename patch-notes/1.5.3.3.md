# TapCatForever 1.5.3.3

**Chests**
- Every chest you open shows the loot box with your item when the reel ends again, whatever its quality.

**Realm leaderboard**
- Your own cat always shows on the leaderboard once you joined, also on realms whose names have dots, brackets or other characters the leaderboard didn't expect. Players on those realms can now share with each other too.
- If something goes wrong while the leaderboard draws, it now says so on the tab (and once in chat) instead of showing an empty board.
