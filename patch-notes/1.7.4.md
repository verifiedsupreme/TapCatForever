# TapCatForever 1.7.4
Companion cats: 56 real WoW cats to collect, buy or earn in dungeons, one per cat, walking beside yours. Plus a snazzier Shop and a faster Heroic Cache.

**Companion cats**
- Your cat can have a companion: a real WoW cat, the game's own 3D model. There are 56 to collect: house cats, kittens and cubs, lynxes, sabers, tigers, lions, panthers, the Felsaber, the Spectral Tiger and more.
- They're a long-term goal. House cats start at 50,000 TapCoins, kittens and cubs cost 100,000 to 150,000, and the big cats go up to 500,000. The glowing, fel and spectral cats cost up to 1,000,000.
- Eleven can't be bought. Clear big dungeons and raids in Adventure to earn them, from an Alley Cat in the Deadmines to Mr. Bigglesworth in Naxxramas.
- The companions you own are for all your cats, and each class's cat chooses its own one.
- A new companion slot on the character panel (right of the ranged slot) shows yours beside your cat. Click it, or type /tcf companions, to choose or buy one.
- **Companion Stroll** is the biggest buy in the game at 1,000,000 TapCoins. Your companion walks back and forth beside your cat on screen, opposite the party cats and at their size. You can turn it off any time in the companion window.
- Your companion has a life of its own:
  - It wanders, sits down and sometimes curls up for a nap.
  - On a long tapping streak it gets curious and comes a few steps closer to watch.
  - When you pet your cat, it comes right over, sits and keeps its eyes on your cat, and hops when your cat purrs.

**A snazzier Shop**
- The Shop has three views: Featured, Cat Skins and Chests, plus a Companions button.
- **Featured:**
  - The Heroic Cache up front, with its odds and its top prizes.
  - Your Daily Wares.
  - A discounted **Skin of the Day** (20% off), your racial heritage piece and the companion cats.
- **Cat Skins:** every shop skin in one grid, to buy, wear or try on. Filter by For sale, Owned or All skins.
- **Chests:** every chest with its odds, and your Treasure Bag.
- Daily Wares only offer class pieces your cat can wear.

**Heroic Cache**
- It costs 1,600 TapCoins now, and gives a little more Epic gear (about 40% of opens, up from 37%).
- The reel spins faster and shows a mix of different items, mostly Rares and Epics, with no repeats on screen.
- The best prizes (Legendaries and raid Epics) show up on the reel too. Below the level they need they're locked, with the level on them, so you can see what's waiting for you.
- What a cat can really win doesn't change:
  - gear near its level
  - a real Legendary only at level 60, about 1 in 500
