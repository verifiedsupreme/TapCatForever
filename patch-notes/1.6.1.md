# TapCatForever 1.6.1

Fixes for the multiplayer problems players reported in 1.6.0. Multiplayer is still in the works, and we would like to hear whether the death roll works for you now (/tcf feedback).

**Casino: death roll and rock paper scissors with a friend**
- A death roll or rock paper scissors game with a friend no longer stops with "is no longer in your group" right after the challenge is accepted. The game hides the group's names from addons, so TapCatForever no longer asks the game whether your friend is still there: it knows from your friend's own addon. Your friend counts as gone only when their addon has been quiet and the group has really changed, with a few seconds of grace. When the game does show your friend's name in the group, that confirms they are there.
- A friend on a loading screen (a hearth, a portal, a zone line) no longer ends the game: the game reports them as offline for a moment, and the same few seconds of grace apply before the game is called off and the bets come back.
- A roll result the game hides from the addon now calls the death roll off with your bet back, instead of waiting for the turn timer.

**Party cats**
- A friend's profile (click their cat in the party row) now shows their total taps, achievements, zones cleared and whether they are on the leaderboard, above their gear.
- Party cats tell each other their stats along with their look, at most once a minute and only when something changed. Older versions ignore the extra information. A cat wearing many long-named crafted cosmetics sends its gear first and leaves out what does not fit, so nothing that 1.6.0 showed goes missing because of the stats.

**Realm leaderboard**
- The leaderboard fills in from the players in your party or raid: a friend who has joined the leaderboard shows up on yours (and you on theirs) as soon as you see their cat in the party row. Players who have not joined stay off the board, and a friend who leaves the leaderboard comes off yours the same way.
- Your own row never stays in the saved board under your name when one of your own party messages comes back before the addon knows your name (after a /reload).
- /tcf lbdebug now also says how many rows came from party messages and why messages were dropped. Send it to the developer if players still don't show up.

**Other**
- The What's new box's Read the full patch notes button opens the notes of the version the box is about; the 1.6.1 notes sit beside them.
