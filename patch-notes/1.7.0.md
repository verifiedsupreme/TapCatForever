# TapCatForever 1.7.0
Professions 2.0: rare finds and glints by hand, named helpers, guilds with work orders, specializations, a Renown shop, a Profession Bag and 11 legendary crafted sets.

**Professions 2.0: a reason to work your spots by hand**
- The "By hand" buttons no longer show your tool's level on every row.
- Rare finds: clicking a gathering spot by hand can find something rare, better at the later spots. Ore: Black Diamond, Star Ruby, Blood of the Mountain. Leather: Warbear Leather, Black Dragonscale, Pristine Hide of the Beast. Cloth: Essence of Undeath, Demonic Rune, Righteous Orb. Helpers never find them.
- Glints: now and then a spot's tool sparkles. Click it by hand in time for 5 times the reward and a combo; every glint in a row makes rare finds likelier.
- Spot events while you watch your spots: a stash to grab with 10 quick clicks, a rich spot that pays 3 times as much for 30 seconds, or a patrol to hide from (don't click, and your helpers work twice as fast for a minute).
- Your helpers have names and traits now, and bring more goods as they level up, all the way to level 20.
- The gathering spots show your materials exactly (701,620 Linen Cloth instead of 701.62K) up to 10 million, so every click shows.

**Professions 2.0: deeper layers (the new Guild page)**
- Specializations at 225, like Classic: Mooncloth, Shadoweave or Spellfire Tailoring, Armorsmith or Weaponsmith, Dragonscale or Tribal Leatherworking. Each has its own perk and daily craft (Primal Mooncloth, Shadowcloth, Spellcloth, Enchanted Thorium, Arcanite, Cured Rugged Hide, Enchanted Leather).
- Guilds and work orders: The Silken Thread, the Thorium Brotherhood and the Hide and Hunt Lodge post orders for materials, crafted gear, rare finds and daily materials. Orders pay Trade Gold, TapCoins and reputation, from Initiate to Grand Master. Grand Master comes with a title.
- Masterwork: crafted gear now and then comes out as a Masterwork version with better stats.
- Renown shop: selling your business also gives Renown points. Spend them on perks that stay forever: Sharp Tools, Keen Eye, Glint Seeker, Busy Hands, Guild Ties, Rested Hands and Steady Hands. Cats that already have Renown start with points.
- Titles: click your cat's title in the window to show one you earned.

**Renown and unlocks**
- Herbalism, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking now open with the Trade Gold your cat earned since its last sale, not all the Trade Gold it ever earned. Selling your business closes them again until you earn it back, which goes faster with every bit of Renown. Your garden, skills, potions and the rest are kept for when they open again.
- Gathering skill (Mining, Skinning, Cloth Scavenging) rises a third as fast now, and starts over when you sell. With this patch every cat keeps a third of its gathering skill. Your best skill ever still counts for opening the later spots, and spots you already opened stay open.
- The Renown page says plainly what selling does to your money: your bonus now, your bonus after selling, and how much more money that is.

**Profession Bag**
- A new Bag button on the Professions page shows every material your cat has, by tab (Gathered, Rare, Daily, Herbs, Fish & Food, Bandages). Hover one to see what it's used for, and shift-click to sell a whole stack for Trade Gold.
- Bags hold stacks of a set size: a Linen Bag holds 20,000 of each. Every kind always fits, and anything past the size is sold for Trade Gold right away, so nothing is wasted. Piles you already have stay as they are.
- Buy bigger bags with TapCoins, all the way from the Wool Bag to the Bottomless Bag (no limit at all). Bags count for all your cats.

**TapCoins**
- The TapCoin is orange now, so it doesn't look like Trade Gold.
- A gentle auto-clicker guard: if one key is pressed at a perfectly steady, machine-like rhythm for a long run, it stops counting until the rhythm changes. Nothing is taken away, and fast mashing by a real person never trips it.

**Legendary profession sets**
- 11 new animated sets you craft over a long time. Tailoring: Mooncloth Regalia (moonlight and stars), Felweave Shroud (fel fire) and Spellfire (arcane flames). Blacksmithing: Sulfuron Forgemaster (a molten forge) and Dark Iron Bulwark (smouldering dark iron). Leatherworking: Black Dragonscale and Devilsaur Hunter. Herbalism: Lotus Bloom. Fishing: Pinchy's Wish. Cooking: Feast of the Ages. First Aid: Field Medic.
- Their titles: Moonweaver, Felweaver, Spellfire Savant, Forgemaster, Dark Iron Warden, Dragonscale Stalker, Devilsaur Hunter, Lotus Keeper, Angler of Legend, Master Chef and Field Medic.
- Each set takes a long time on purpose: skill 300, your specialization, a high guild rank, many daily materials and rare finds. Owning a whole set gives its title.
