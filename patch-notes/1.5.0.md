# TapCatForever 1.5.0
Class professions with crafted gear, party cats with a tap bonus, a fishing dock, 33 new cat skins and the Compendium.

**Professions by class**
- Your cat's class now picks its main profession. Warriors and Paladins get Mining with Blacksmithing. Rogues, Druids, Hunters and Shamans get Skinning with Leatherworking. Mages, Priests and Warlocks get Tailoring.
- Each one is the same idle business with the same numbers under its own names. Mining has Veins, a Pickaxe and Foremen. Skinning has Hunting Grounds (real beasts from higher-level zones, from Barrens Plainstriders to Devilsaurs), a Skinning Knife and Trappers. Tailoring has Cloth Spots (Defias Hideout, Syndicate Camp, Bloodsail Camp and more), Shears and Scavengers.
- Hunting Grounds and Cloth Spots show the real creatures in 3D.
- Each class's profession keeps its own progress, and your hired helpers keep working, online and offline, even while your cat is a different class.
- Herbalism, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking still unlock later for everyone, at the same Renown costs.
- Existing characters keep their progress: on first login a cloth or leather class moves its Mining spots, tool level, upgrades, milestones and skill to its new profession, and its ore becomes cloth or leather of the same tier.

**Crafting real gear**
- Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring craft real Classic gear with stats, 45 recipes each from skill 1 to 300. Blacksmiths make plate, mail, weapons and shields, leatherworkers make leather and mail, tailors make cloth armor.
- Crafting skill rises the classic way: orange recipes always give a point, yellow often, green sometimes, grey never. Reaching 300 is a long-term goal of several weeks.
- Recipes you can't make yet are listed with the skill they need, so you can see what comes next.
- Every cosmetic recipe says "Cosmetic only (no stats)" in the recipe list, its tooltip and the Compendium. You can't craft a cosmetic you already own.
- Leatherworking and Tailoring each get 5 cosmetic recipes and their own craftable cat skins.
- Crafted gear goes into your current class's gear bag and its tooltip says which profession made it. It is never found as loot, and it sells for Trade Gold, not TapCoins.

**Play with friends**
- Friends in your party or raid who also have TapCatForever show up as small cats in a row beside yours. Hover one to see whose cat it is.
- Your taps count 20% more with one of them and 10% more for each one after that, up to +50% with a full party: more tap progress, cat XP and TapCoins from tapping.
- The addons find each other on their own, across realms, and reconnect after a reload or a fight.

**Fishing dock**
- The Fishing page is now a cartoon dock over a lake. Your cat sits on the dock with a fishing pole and the bobber floats on the water.
- Once you hire a Fishing Buddy, he sits beside your cat with his own pole, and wears a fishing hat once he levels up.

**New cat skins**
- 33 new cat skins, each with its own fur pattern in the cartoon style.
- In the Shop: Mittens, Tuxedo Cat, Cow Cat, Russian Blue, Dalmatian Cat, Tortoiseshell and Snowshoe (300-650 TapCoins), Bengal, Maine Coon and Strawberry Sweetheart (1,000-1,200), and Galaxy Cat (3,200).
- Rare Adventure boss drops (2-5% per kill): Ragefire Cinder Cat, Defias Bandit Cat, Stone Watcher Cat, Maraudon Crystal Cat, Scarlet Crusader Cat, Wind-Up Clockwork Cat, Onyxian Whelpling Cat, Qiraji Scarab Cat and Patchwork Kitty.
- Chest-only cats: Lucky Clover Cat and Rainbow Cat (Adventurer's Strongbox), Golden Treasure Cat and Diamond Cat (Heroic Cache).
- Quest cats (Quest-Giver Cat, Ready-to-Turn-In Cat), expedition cats (Emberheart Cat, Runebound Cat), real dungeon and raid cats (Party Animal, Raid Leader Cat) and craftable cats.

**Compendium**
- A new Compendium window (button at the top of the main window, or /tcf compendium) lists every skin, cosmetic, tap effect, effect and piece of gear, what you own, and how to get the rest.

**Loot and gear**
- Gear and cosmetics you find no longer open a big loot box. They go straight into your bags and a small notice appears by your cat: hover it to compare with what you wear, click it to open the Gear or Appearance tab, right-click to dismiss. Opening a chest still plays the reel.
- Every gear tooltip compares the item with what your cat is wearing, as a Power change.
- "Sell unequipped" is now "Sell junk": it sells gear that would lower your Power and extra copies you can't wear. Upgrades are kept, even ones you need a higher level for.
- Starter weapons fit the class: a bow for hunters, a dagger for rogues, a mace for paladins, priests and shamans, a sword for warriors and a staff for mages, warlocks and druids.
