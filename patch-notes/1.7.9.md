**A sixth Legendary: Iverson's Legendary Mullet**
- A big pink mullet for your cat's head, for Mages, Priests and Warlocks. Business in the front, party in the back.
- Like the other one of a kind Legendaries, it's level 60 and comes from a Heroic Cache or Raider's Hoard opened by a level 60 cat.
- It has its own look and effects: pink hearts floating off it and party confetti spilling off the back.
