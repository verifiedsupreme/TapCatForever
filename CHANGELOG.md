# Changelog

## 1.4.0

**More enemies in Adventure**
- Every zone, dungeon and raid now has its real Classic creatures: up to 16 different enemies per zone (335 in all, up from 56), with real models taken from a full Classic creature database. Molten Core goes from 2 creature types to 13, Naxxramas from 3 to 15, Elwynn Forest from 3 to 13.
- More bosses now use their own real models instead of stand-ins.
- A pack no longer has the same creature twice when the zone has enough variety.
- Enemies play their death animation and fade out (including the last one in a pack and bosses), and killing blows show their spell effect.
- Damage and healing numbers float above enemies instead of behind them, and the ability buttons flash when your cat uses an ability.

**Adventure fixes and balance**
- Fixed a bug that kept Divine Shield, Ice Block, Recklessness and other buffs up almost all the time and reset every cooldown at the start of each fight. Abilities now last and recharge as their tooltips say, so some raid bosses are harder.
- Adventure chests are now based on time spent fighting: about one Cat Treat Pouch per 15-25 minutes in zones and dungeons, and one Adventurer's Strongbox per 30 minutes in raids, dropped by a boss. Trash no longer drops pouches.
- Auto-advance off now really means "keep farming this stage": a lost fight retries the same stage. After 3 losses in a row your cat drops back to an easier pack. With Auto-advance on, a cat that loses at the start of a zone falls back to the previous zone.
- Bandages in fights work again after their cooldown, instead of staying locked.

**Gear and chests**
- A chest's Rare or Epic roll no longer quietly turns into a green item; the chest looks a little wider for your level before dropping a quality.
- Chests keep giving proper gear after your cat passes level 60.
- Brand-new cats no longer get very strong endgame gear: items with no level requirement now need roughly their item level minus 5. Every class starts with a fair level-10 weapon.
- Boss drops are always gear your class can wear. Blizzard's leftover test items are gone from loot.
- Equipping a one-hander while wearing a two-hander now replaces the two-hander. With both ring or trinket slots full, Equip replaces the weaker one. The "+power" number is now correct in every case, and you can equip a second copy of a ring, trinket or one-hander.
- Selling: tooltips say exactly what Sell does, you're asked before selling your only copy of a rare or epic, and "Sell unequipped" no longer stutters with a big bag.
- Hovering gear previews it on your cat even when that slot has a cosmetic.
- The Shop shows the real chest odds (90% gear, 10% cosmetic).

**Cartoon cat**
- Hunter bows, guns, crossbows and thrown weapons now show in your cat's paw, and capes show on its back in their own colours.
- Gear that shares another slot's icon now draws in the right place (no more belts on helms or daggers from necklaces).
- Floating effects bob smoothly and stay centred.
- Scrolling over the cat zooms the camera again (hold Ctrl or Shift to resize or fade the cat).
- The cat no longer leaves an invisible strip that blocked clicks, and it stays exactly where you put it after this update.

**Professions**
- Pickaxe swings on higher veins now pay a fair share of that vein's output instead of beating your whole business.
- Mr. Pinchy's gold wish now gives about 10 minutes of what your mines make. Your fishing buddy finds treasure much less often than you do when you fish yourself, and lists offline finds in one line.
- Cooking: ingredients taken from the pot before you cook now empty the pot (no more free dishes), Burnt Mess rarely raises your skill, and there's a new recipe to discover that uses Raptor Flesh.
- First Aid: the Field Medic makes the best bandage you have cloth for, and stalled queues say what cloth they need.
- Herbalism: clicking a locked plot no longer buys a different plot.

**Expeditions, quests and dungeons**
- Short expeditions are now mainly for XP and TapCoins; chests come mostly from the long ones. Tooltips show the real chest odds.
- You're told when a party gets back (on the cat, and in chat if chat messages are on), even if it came back while you were offline.
- Quests track progress correctly across your characters and can't show negative numbers. Boss quests take about as long as the others.
- Real dungeon and raid chests: re-entering after stepping out or a wipe continues the same run. Each dungeon gives a chest once a day, each raid once a week, and up to 2 dungeon chests a day across your characters. You're told when a run gives no chest.

**Account and characters**
- Achievement rewards (cosmetics and cat skins such as Mr. Bigglesworth) now appear on all your characters, and you keep them after "Reset this character".
- "Citizen of Azeroth" counts heritage pieces from all your characters, and "Completionist" can reach 100% again.
- Each character has its own Daily Wares.
- Playing past midnight gives you the new day's treat and keeps your streak.
- Slash commands and replies to your own clicks always show in chat, even with "Chat messages" turned off; that setting now only mutes passive notices.

**Interface**
- The Treasure Bag is wider so chest names aren't hidden, the loot box switches to a new reel's item instead of hiding it, and "Open all" no longer freezes the game with a big pile of chests.
- Achievements show their cat-skin rewards, and the Appearance and Shop pages no longer overlap their buttons.

## 1.3.3
- The loot box only pops up while the TapCatForever window or Treasure Bag is open; otherwise drops show as a small note above the cat.
- "Sell spare copies" is now "Sell unequipped": it says it sells every piece of gear the cat isn't wearing, and asks you to confirm with the item count and TapCoins.
- Loot box: Open another, Equip, OK to all and OK no longer overlap.
- Welcome: the tip on the second page no longer hides behind the Back button.

## 1.3.2
- Adventure: you can move on to the next zone as soon as you beat the current one, whatever your level (Auto-advance still waits for the right level).
- Every boss drops a guaranteed piece of its real loot the first time you beat it.
- Adventure shows what the next zone needs next to the zone arrows (clear the final boss, or the level required).
- Shorter welcome: two pages, the logo with your tapping cat, then a picture grid of what to do.
- Pickaxe swings now grow with the deposit: each swing is worth half a second of everything that vein produces (all your mines there, with milestones and upgrades), never less than a quarter of one mine's cycle. Pickaxe levels add 15% each.

## 1.3.1
- Chests now give real gear 90% of the time (cosmetics 10%).
- Cosmetics are clearly marked: a COSMETIC tag and "appearance only, no stats" in the loot box and tooltips, and a purple badge on the chest reel. Gear shows a GEAR tag.

## 1.3.0
- **Hand-drawn Adventure backgrounds** for all 28 zones, dungeons and raids.
- **Chest opening reel**: possible loot spins by and lands on your reward (can be turned off in Settings).
- Mining: idle veins say "Click to mine a cycle" and pulse; the pickaxe is labelled "By hand".
- **Spell effects in Adventure**: your cat's abilities now show as they're cast: fireballs, frostbolts, shadow bolts, lightning, arrows, slashes, falling Blizzard and Rain of Fire, heals, shield bubbles and stun stars.
- **Ability bar** under the fight with every learned ability and its cooldown counting down.
- Chests now only give cosmetics your current class can wear (its own sets or universal items).
- Welcome tour on first login (also in Settings and /tcf welcome), with a cartoon version of the TapCatForever logo.
- Hand mining pays much less: a quarter of one mine's cycle per swing, with a 25% chance of ore.

## 1.2.1
- Developer test shortcuts are now password-locked.

## 1.2.0

- **New cartoon cat**: a hand-drawn sticker-style cat with expressions (happy, blink, sparkle, focus). It replaces the pixel cat.
- **Every visible item has its own drawing**: all ~4,500 helms, chests, belts, necklaces, rings, weapons, shields and off-hand items, each drawn from its in-game icon (785 unique looks).
- Dual-wielded weapons appear in both paws; shields, books and orbs in the left paw.
- Hovering an item now shows your cat wearing it (instead of your WoW character).
- Treasure Bag: "All" button per chest type and "Open everything".
- The level and XP text now sit above the cat (Settings: "Text above the cat"). Chat messages and sounds now start off.
- Mining veins only run when you start them or a foreman works them (key presses no longer restart them).
- Each cat class has its own gear bag: gear found on one class no longer shows up for the others (an old shared bag is split once: worn items stay with their class, the rest go to the class you are playing).
- **All cosmetics redrawn for the cartoon cat**: every hat, chest, cape and wings, weapon, off-hand, accessory and racial heritage item (78 designs), tinted in each item's own colours. Fur patterns recolour the cat's markings.
- **Fur patterns and special skins drawn properly**: tabby, tuxedo, star blaze, calico, Siamese points and leopard markings; tiger stripes, star spots, Mechanocat plates, Skeletal Cat bones, the War Lion's mane and coloured eyes.
- **Mining**: one pickaxe for every vein with an endless upgrade (+15% per hand-mined click per level, an extra ore every 15 levels). Clicking the pickaxe shows what you mined; it has a 1.5s swing cooldown (shorter with upgrades, down to 0.75s).
- **Herbalism**: seeds show their price (or the skill they need), the garden says what you're planting and what it costs, and failed plants explain why.
- Professions page: buttons no longer miss clicks (the page was redrawing its views underneath the mouse).
- Cooking: spice buttons show their price, and the tooltip explains what spices are for and which known dishes use them.
- Expeditions give XP to every cat that went (5 XP per minute away, half on a failure).
- **Tap Effects**: a new cosmetic slot. A little burst pops from the paw on every tap: 17 to collect (Dust Puffs and Paw Prints up to Holy Light and Rainbow Stars), from chests and the shop.
- Paws move further on each tap. The chest badge next to the cat is gone (open chests from the Treasure Bag button in the window).
- Settings: "Reset this character" and "Reset ALL progress".
- **Real WoW gear**: 8,661 real Classic items with their real stats (Strength, Stamina, Armor, weapon DPS, crit, spell power...), drop sources and drop chances. 17 gear slots per class (Head to Ranged), Classic armor and weapon rules per class, and the cat wears its real gear (cosmetics still override the look).
- **Adventure rebuilt around Azeroth**: 28 real zones, dungeons and raids in level order (Elwynn Forest, Deadmines, Scarlet Monastery... Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Naxxramas) with their real bosses and real loot tables. Packs of 1-3 enemies shown as 3D creature models, over each dungeon's own artwork. Zones unlock by class level; raids need raid gear.
- **Class abilities**: 9-10 real abilities per class (Heroic Strike, Fireball, Frost Nova, Shadow Word: Pain, Holy Light...) bought with TapCoins and used automatically in fights.
- **Expeditions**: send a party of 3 cats away for 15 minutes to 24 hours (even offline); they come back with TapCoins, chests and gear.
- **Professions**: five professions that each play differently, all with an idle mode, unlocked with Renown (selling your business): **Mining** (free: an idle tycoon of veins with foremen and milestones, plus your own pickaxe to mine by hand), **Herbalism** (5 Renown: a garden whose plots grow in real time; key presses water it; a gardener harvests and replants, and rare Black Lotus mutations), **First Aid** (20: cloth from Adventure becomes the 10 Classic bandages, which heal your cat mid-fight; a field medic and cloth contract automate it), **Fishing** (60: a bobber mini-game across five real fishing spots with Mr. Pinchy's wishes; a fishing buddy catches on his own) and **Cooking** (150: experiment with fish, meat, herbs and spices to discover 12 Classic dishes that give Well Fed buffs; a sous-chef cooks for you). Materials still craft exclusive effects and cats.
- **Cat skins**: a new Skin slot that restyles the whole cat: Striped Frostsaber, Striped Nightsaber, Spotted Frostsaber, Black Panther, Darkmoon Kitty, Winterspring Frostsaber, Mechanical Kitty, Fel Kitty, Golden Lion and Spectral Tiger (bought with TapCoins in the Appearance tab, or rarely found in Heroic Caches), plus Swift Zulian Tiger (High Priest Thekal) and Skeletal Cat (Baron Rivendare) as rare Adventure boss drops.
- **Racial heritage**: buy your character's race accessory in the Shop (Orc tusks, Tauren horns, Night Elf ears, Gnome goggles, Skyborne feathers and more); it can be hidden in Settings.
- Real dungeons: below level 20 you get a Cat Treat Pouch, from 20 an Adventurer's Strongbox (chance of a Heroic Cache, higher from 50); raids give a Heroic Cache.
- Chests now hold real gear (65%) or cosmetics (35%). Epic and Legendary gear is deliberately rare.
- Appearance only shows cosmetics you've unlocked.
- **Gear try-on**: hover gear to see your cat wearing it.
- **Per-character progress**: gear, cosmetics, skins, chests, professions, cat levels, abilities and Adventure belong to each character; TapCoins, quests, streak and achievements are shared.
- Adventure waits for you to press Start; only the selected class's cat fights. Rewards are mostly chests. Every enemy is shown as a model, and Elwynn Forest and Westfall have painted scenes.
- Loot from chests and bosses suits your class (no caster staves for Warriors). The loot box shows what else is waiting and has "OK to all".
- More to collect: 104 colour variants of 13 effect styles, and cat skins from tabbies to sabers and achievement cats like Mr. Bigglesworth.
- New "Text size" setting, separate from cat size, so a tiny cat can keep readable text.
- New quests and achievements for gear, expeditions and crafting.

- **Adventure**: a new idle game tab. Your cat fights through 12 tiers of classic zones, dungeons and raids (Elwynn Forest to Naxxramas, then endless Heroic tiers), using the gear equipped on the current class. Every key press lands a bonus paw strike. Bosses drop cosmetics.
- **Quests**: 3 daily quests (TapCoins + a Cat Treat Pouch each) and a weekly quest (TapCoins + an Adventurer's Strongbox).
- **Real dungeon and raid rewards**: boss kills pay TapCoins; finishing a dungeon gives an Adventurer's Strongbox, a raid gives a Heroic Cache.
- **Treasure Bag** for unopened chests, and a **loot reveal box** that shows what you got.
- **Per-class levels**: each class levels up on its own, starting at 1.
- **Gear slots** around the cat in the window: click a slot to choose its item, right-click to empty it.
- A / left arrow and left mouse tap the left paw; D / right arrow and right mouse tap the right paw; other keys alternate.
- Weapons, shields and other held items now sit in front of the paws.
- The cat in the window no longer taps on its own.
- TapCoins are scarcer: 1 per 50 key presses; quests and Adventure are the main income. Daily wares show 3 items.
