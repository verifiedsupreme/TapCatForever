# Changelog

## 1.7.0

**Professions 2.0: a reason to work your spots by hand**
- The "By hand" buttons no longer show your tool's level on every row.
- Rare finds: clicking a gathering spot by hand can find something rare, better at the later spots. Ore: Black Diamond, Star Ruby, Blood of the Mountain. Leather: Warbear Leather, Black Dragonscale, Pristine Hide of the Beast. Cloth: Essence of Undeath, Demonic Rune, Righteous Orb. Helpers never find them.
- Glints: now and then a spot's tool sparkles. Click it by hand in time for 5 times the reward and a combo; every glint in a row makes rare finds likelier.
- Spot events while you watch your spots: a stash to grab with 10 quick clicks, a rich spot that pays 3 times as much for 30 seconds, or a patrol to hide from (don't click, and your helpers work twice as fast for a minute).
- Your helpers have names and traits now, and bring more goods as they level up, all the way to level 20.
- The gathering spots show your materials exactly (701,620 Linen Cloth instead of 701.62K) up to 10 million, so every click shows.

**Professions 2.0: deeper layers (the new Guild page)**
- Specializations at 225, like Classic: Mooncloth, Shadoweave or Spellfire Tailoring, Armorsmith or Weaponsmith, Dragonscale or Tribal Leatherworking. Each has its own perk and daily craft (Primal Mooncloth, Shadowcloth, Spellcloth, Enchanted Thorium, Arcanite, Cured Rugged Hide, Enchanted Leather).
- Guilds and work orders: The Silken Thread, the Thorium Brotherhood and the Hide and Hunt Lodge post orders for materials, crafted gear, rare finds and daily materials. Orders pay Trade Gold, TapCoins and reputation, from Initiate to Grand Master. Grand Master comes with a title.
- Masterwork: crafted gear now and then comes out as a Masterwork version with better stats.
- Renown shop: selling your business also gives Renown points. Spend them on perks that stay forever: Sharp Tools, Keen Eye, Glint Seeker, Busy Hands, Guild Ties, Rested Hands and Steady Hands. Cats that already have Renown start with points.
- Titles: click your cat's title in the window to show one you earned.

**Renown and unlocks**
- Herbalism, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking now open with the Trade Gold your cat earned since its last sale, not all the Trade Gold it ever earned. Selling your business closes them again until you earn it back, which goes faster with every bit of Renown. Your garden, skills, potions and the rest are kept for when they open again.
- Gathering skill (Mining, Skinning, Cloth Scavenging) rises a third as fast now, and starts over when you sell. With this patch every cat keeps a third of its gathering skill. Your best skill ever still counts for opening the later spots, and spots you already opened stay open.
- The Renown page says plainly what selling does to your money: your bonus now, your bonus after selling, and how much more money that is.

**Profession Bag**
- A new Bag button on the Professions page shows every material your cat has, by tab (Gathered, Rare, Daily, Herbs, Fish & Food, Bandages). Hover one to see what it's used for, and shift-click to sell a whole stack for Trade Gold.
- Bags hold stacks of a set size: a Linen Bag holds 20,000 of each. Every kind always fits, and anything past the size is sold for Trade Gold right away, so nothing is wasted. Piles you already have stay as they are.
- Buy bigger bags with TapCoins, all the way from the Wool Bag to the Bottomless Bag (no limit at all). Bags count for all your cats.

**TapCoins**
- The TapCoin is orange now, so it doesn't look like Trade Gold.
- A gentle auto-clicker guard: if one key is pressed at a perfectly steady, machine-like rhythm for a long run, it stops counting until the rhythm changes. Nothing is taken away, and fast mashing by a real person never trips it.

**Legendary profession sets**
- 11 new animated sets you craft over a long time. Tailoring: Mooncloth Regalia (moonlight and stars), Felweave Shroud (fel fire) and Spellfire (arcane flames). Blacksmithing: Sulfuron Forgemaster (a molten forge) and Dark Iron Bulwark (smouldering dark iron). Leatherworking: Black Dragonscale and Devilsaur Hunter. Herbalism: Lotus Bloom. Fishing: Pinchy's Wish. Cooking: Feast of the Ages. First Aid: Field Medic.
- Their titles: Moonweaver, Felweaver, Spellfire Savant, Forgemaster, Dark Iron Warden, Dragonscale Stalker, Devilsaur Hunter, Lotus Keeper, Angler of Legend, Master Chef and Field Medic.
- Each set takes a long time on purpose: skill 300, your specialization, a high guild rank, many daily materials and rare finds. Owning a whole set gives its title.

**Tapping faces**

Hire it, then train it from Apprentice to Journeyman, Expert, Artisan and Master: plants grow faster, one replant in three is free, one more herb from every harvest, and twice the rare herbs. Bramble also plants empty plots for you. All of this has its own Gardener page next to Garden.

Use the arrows (or the mouse wheel) to page through the seeds.

**Potions**
- Herbalism's Crafting list now brews 17 potions from your herbs, from Elixir of Lion's Strength to Flask of the Titans, Flask of Supreme Power and Flask of Distilled Wisdom (those need a Black Lotus).
- Drink one for a buff: Adventure damage or health, cat XP, gathering profits, rare fish or garden growth. One potion at a time, and it counts on top of a Well Fed dish. Brewing raises your Herbalism skill too.

**Rock paper scissors: best of 3 and best of 5**
- A new button in the top bar picks best of 1, 3 or 5, against the house cat or a friend. In best of 3 the first to win 2 rounds takes the game, in best of 5 the first to win 3. Ties still play again and don't count.
- The score shows in the top bar while the game runs, and each round won gets a small cheer. The bet is the same for the whole game: one bet, one payout.
- Your friend needs this version for best of 3 or 5 (the game tells you if they don't have it yet). Best of 1 works with every version.

**Crafting**
- The button next to Craft now says how many it makes ("x12", "x50") instead of "All", since one click makes at most 50.

**Settings**
- New Show party cats box: turn it off to hide the row of party members' cats beside yours. It only hides the picture: your taps still count more with friends, and death roll, rock paper scissors and party expeditions still work.

**Your cats on every character**

Any character plays the account's cat of its class (or the one you pick), with its level, XP, gear and bags, Treasure Bag, professions, look and cosmetics, Adventure progress and farming, abilities and expeditions.

- A character that logs in later with cats of its own (for example one you played on an older version) is put together the same way.
- A new character starts on the account's cat of its class.
- Still per character: which cat it plays, the Shop's Daily Wares, real WoW dungeon and raid chest limits, and how many times you killed a real WoW boss. TapCoins, quests, achievements and settings were already shared.
- "Reset this character" in Settings is now "Reset your cats": it starts all nine cats over, for every character. TapCoins, quests and achievements are kept.

**Casino**
- New: the Casino (a new tab between Shop and Leaderboard), with Lucky Paws slots, blackjack, and death roll and rock paper scissors against a house cat or a friend in your party. It fills the whole window under the tabs: pick Slots, Blackjack or Death roll with the buttons at the top (the Death roll view also plays rock paper scissors).
- In the Friend view a short line under the top bar says whether the friend you picked can play: "Ready to play", "Checking their TapCatForever..." or "Their TapCatForever didn't answer (they need the newest version)". Party cats work on older versions too, so you can see a friend's cat while their addon can't take a challenge yet.
- Challenges between friends now reach the right player even when the game doesn't let addons read your own name: each addon learns the name the game gives you from your own messages.
- Right after you join a group your addon may not know your name yet: a challenge then says so, and works a few seconds later.
- The Casino's hellos between addons are kept to one hello and at most one answer each when the group changes, so they never hold up duel messages in a big raid.
- /tcf name says whether the game lets addons read your name and which name TapCatForever learned for you.
- The line at the top shows your bet limits, and your stats for the game you are playing: games played, total bet, paid back, net and your biggest win (hover it for every game).

**Casino: bets**
- A bet is at least 10 TapCoins and at most half your TapCoins (rounded down to the bet step). There is no top bet. You need at least 20 TapCoins to play.
- The - and + buttons take bigger steps on big bets (100 from 1,000, 250 from 2,500, 500 from 5,000, and so on).
- In a duel with a friend, each of you can bet at most half your own TapCoins, and exactly the bet you both agreed moves between you.
- Big bets show in short in the bet box, for example 125.5K or 4.5M.
- Use the - and + buttons or the quick 10, 50, 100 and Max buttons to choose your bet. All the games share the same bet, and it is kept for next time.
- Every game against the house keeps a small house edge, so over time the Casino takes a few TapCoins out of the game. Duels with friends are even: no table fee. It is for fun, not for getting rich. There are no free spins.
- Casino bets and wins don't count toward the TapCoins earned and spent achievements.

**Casino: Lucky Paws slots**
- A cartoon cat slot machine with three reels. Pull the lever or press Spin. The reels spin, slow down and stop from left to right with a little bounce. Click the reels (or press Stop) to land them at once.
- Only the middle line counts. Three of a kind wins, and every cat on the line pays something.
- Pays (times your bet, your bet included): three cats 100, three gems 40, three bells 25, three coins 15, three fish 10, three yarn balls 5, three paws 3, two cats 5, one cat 2.
- The rarer a line, the more it pays: three cats come up about 1 in 1,000 spins, gems 1 in 667, bells 1 in 500, coins 1 in 400, fish 1 in 296, yarn balls 1 in 222 and paws 1 in 178. Hover the "How it works" line for the chance of every line.
- The result line says what you won on top of your bet: "One cat. You win 10 TapCoins" on a bet of 10 (you get 20 back).
- About 3 spins in 10 win something. Over many spins the machine pays back about 94% of what is bet.
- A big win (10 times your bet or more) gets a coin shower, flashing lights and a cheer from your cat, inside the window only.

**Casino: blackjack**
- Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Picture cards count 10, aces 1 or 11.
- A win pays 1 to 1. A blackjack (an ace and a 10 card as your first two cards) pays 3 to 2, with half a TapCoin rounded down on odd bets. A tie is a push: you get your bet back.
- The dealer draws to 17 and stands on every 17, soft 17 too. The dealer checks for blackjack first.
- Double: double your bet on any first two cards and take exactly one more card. Split: split a pair into two hands once. Split aces get one card each.
- No insurance. Six decks, shuffled again when a quarter of the shoe is left. With perfect play the house keeps about half a percent.
- Closing the window doesn't lose your hand: it waits for you, even after a reload. A win is always paid, even if you close the window while the cards are still being dealt.

**Casino: death roll**
- Two cats take turns rolling. The first roll is 1 to 1,000 (or 100, or 10,000: pick where the rolls start). Each next roll is 1 to the last number rolled. Whoever rolls a 1 loses.
- It plays out in a cosy cartoon cat tavern, by the fire: your cat on the left in its real look, the other cat on the right. Each roll pops up over the roller's head and the range in the middle shrinks. Numbers of 10 and below turn red and shake.
- On a 1 the loser spins and flops onto its back with X eyes, a little ghost cat floats up, and a few seconds later it pops back up, fine. The winner jumps around in confetti.
- A small How to play box above the table explains the rules (hover it to read them as a tooltip too).

**Casino: death roll against the house cat**
- Press House cat, choose your bet and where the rolls start, and press Start. The house cat has a fun name and look each game (Lucky Paws is a tuxedo cat).
- A coin flip decides who rolls first, so you win half the games.
- A win gives you 1.9 times your bet back: on a bet of 100 you win 90. Over many games the house keeps about 5% of what is bet.
- A game you leave halfway waits for you, even after a reload.

**Casino: death roll with a friend**
- Group up (party or raid) with a friend who has TapCatForever. Press Friend, pick them with the arrows, choose a bet and press Challenge.
- Your friend gets a small Accept or Decline box just above their cat on the screen, and in the Casino tab. It goes away after 60 seconds. If you decline or don't answer, that friend can challenge you again after 20 seconds.
- You both need enough TapCoins for the bet (each player's own limits count). Your bet is held while the duel runs and comes back if it is called off.
- The rolls are real /roll rolls: the Roll button rolls for you, and they also show in chat for your group like a normal /roll. You can type /roll yourself too, as long as it is your turn and the right range.
- Whoever rolls the 1 loses their bet, and the winner gets all of it. There is no table fee between friends.
- Each turn has 60 seconds. If you don't roll in time, you lose the duel, the same as rolling the 1.
- If someone reloads or leaves the group before the 1, or the game hides the roll results, the duel is called off and no TapCoins move. Once the 1 has been rolled, the result stands: leaving the group or a reload then doesn't undo it.
- Duels with friends don't work inside dungeons, raids, battlegrounds or arenas: the game hides the roll results there. Challenge each other outside.

**Casino: rock paper scissors**
- Pick Rock paper scissors under the top bar of the Death roll view (Death roll switches back). It plays out on a snowy mountaintop above the clouds, with little flags blowing in the wind.
- Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats rock. Both cats shake a paw three times ("rock, paper, scissors") and then show their sign: a fist paw, an open paw, or two toes up, drawn in each cat's own fur colour. The winner hops in confetti and the loser flops over, like the death roll.
- A small How to play box explains the rules (hover it for a tooltip too).

**Casino: rock paper scissors against the house cat**
- Press House cat, choose your bet and press Rock, Paper or Scissors: that places the bet. The house cat picks at random.
- A tie plays again for free: your bet stays on the table until someone wins.
- A win gives you 1.9 times your bet back: on a bet of 100 you win 90. Ties replay, so you win half the games, and over many games the house keeps about 5% of what is bet, the same as the death roll.
- A game waiting after a tie waits for you, even after a reload.

**Casino: rock paper scissors with a friend**
- Press Friend, pick a friend in your party or raid who has TapCatForever with the arrows, choose a bet and press Challenge. They get the same small Accept or Decline box as for a death roll.
- Best of 1, and a tie plays again. You both bet the same amount, and the winner gets the whole bet (no table fee).
- Neither addon can see the other pick first: each one locks in its pick (it sends only a scrambled code of the pick and a random secret), and only when both are locked in do they show their picks, and each checks the other's matches what was locked in.
- If a pick doesn't match what was locked in, or doesn't arrive within 30 seconds, the game is called off and no TapCoins move. The game says why.
- Each pick has 60 seconds. A reload or leaving the group before the result calls the game off, and no TapCoins move.
- Unlike death roll, it works inside dungeons and raids too: it doesn't use /roll.

**Adventure: enemies above your cat's level**

- Enemies below your cat give it a little more damage: 1% a level, at most 5%.
- An enemy's level is the zone's lowest level for its first packs, up to 2 or 3 levels more for its last boss. Raids count as level 60.
- When the enemies are 5 or more levels above your cat, a line in the top-left corner of the fight, above your cat, says so, for example "Enemies here are 29 levels above your cat: it hits much less and takes far more damage."
