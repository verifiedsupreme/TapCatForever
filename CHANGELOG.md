# Changelog

## 1.5.3

**Levels**
- Cat levels now stop at 60, like Classic. A cat above 60 becomes 60 and keeps its gear, abilities and titles. A cat at 60 no longer gains XP, and its XP bar says Max level.
- Early levels are faster than before: up to about level 30 each level needs less XP. After about 30 each level takes longer than before, up to about twice as long near 60. Playing about 2 hours a day, a new cat reaches level 10 in about an hour and level 60 in a few months.
- Your cats keep the same share of their current level when the new XP amounts come in, so no cat skips levels or loses its progress toward the next one.
- The last two level titles and achievements moved to levels you can reach: Legendary Lap Cat at level 55 and Eternal Tapper at level 60. Achievements you already have are kept.
- The Growing Up daily quest is no longer offered when all your cats are level 60.
- Level-up TapCoins and level chests are now paid once per class and level for your whole account. A new character, or one you reset, gets them again only above the highest level your account already reached with that class. Reset this character says so before you confirm.

**Dungeon Chest**
- New chest: the Dungeon Chest. It always holds gear: boss loot from the Adventure dungeons your cat has reached (Ragefire Chasm if it has reached none), close to your cat's level. Mostly Rare, some Uncommon, a little Epic, never Legendary. When those dungeons have nothing of that quality near your cat's level, it gives gear of that quality from around the world at your cat's level instead.
- The first clear of each dungeon in Adventure gives a Dungeon Chest instead of an Adventurer's Strongbox. After that, finishing a dungeon again gives one about every 2 to 3 hours of dungeon fighting. Open-world zones still give a Strongbox for the first clear.
- Finishing a real WoW dungeon now always gives a Dungeon Chest, instead of a Cat Treat Pouch, Adventurer's Strongbox or Heroic Cache picked by your level. The limits are the same: once a day for each dungeon, and up to 2 a day across your characters.
- The Golden Treasure Cat and Diamond Cat can still turn up after a real WoW dungeon, at the same odds as when dungeons sometimes gave a Heroic Cache.
- The Deadmines, Scarlet Monastery, Blackrock Depths and Stratholme expeditions sometimes bring home a Dungeon Chest (2%, 5%, 10% and 15% when they succeed).
- The Dungeon Chest is not sold in the Shop. The Treasure Bag has a row for it.
- New achievements: Spoils of the Deep and Dungeon Crawler (open 1 and 25 Dungeon Chests).

**Loot**
- Chests now and then hold a few TapCoins on top of their contents. Most hold none, about a third hold a few (2 to 15, by chest) and about 1 in 20 holds a bigger handful (40 to 300, by chest). The loot box shows them as one plain line, such as +12 TapCoins, and a small find from a chest shows them on its slim line.
- Small finds now go to your cat's weapon (a ranged weapon for Hunters) and the gear you can see (head, chest, belt, cloak, neck, rings and off-hand) well ahead of hands, feet, wrists, legs and shoulders.
- Burning Crusade and later items no longer drop anywhere, including Outland quest greens, Gnomish goggles, Jewelcrafting rings and blood elf and draenei starting gear. You keep any you already have.
- Uncommon or better items with no stats at all, such as on-use trinkets like Talisman of Arathor, no longer drop. They did nothing for your cat. You keep any you already have.
- Leftover test items that older versions let drop, such as Warbranch of Cenarius (a blue staff at item level 1 with no stats), no longer drop. Any your cats had are taken off them once, and you get their sell value in TapCoins. A cat left without a weapon gets its class weapon back.

**Gear and looks**
- Every class starts with a weapon of its own: a sword for Warriors, a mace for Paladins, a bow for Hunters, a dagger for Rogues and Warlocks, an axe for Shamans, and a different staff each for Priests, Mages and Druids.
- A cat can always wear its class's starter weapon, whatever the weapon's level. A cat still holding the weapon an older version gave it gets the new one, unless that would push out an off-hand item you chose or lower its Power. Then the new one waits in its bag. Hunters keep their bow, and a weapon you picked yourself is left alone.
- Capes and cloaks now hang down both sides of your cat and tuck in behind it, with no band across the bottom under the paws. Cosmetic capes use the same shape.

**Professions**
- Herbalism, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking now unlock as each cat earns Trade Gold: 25M, 2.5B, 250B and 2T earned in total. Selling your business for Renown never lowers this, and Renown makes the gold come faster. The old Renown amounts still unlock them too, and professions you already unlocked stay unlocked.
- When a cat can learn a new profession you get one chat line, once. A cat you aren't playing hears about it the next time you play it.
- The Professions page shows how much Trade Gold a locked profession needs and how far along this cat is, for example So far: 5.00M (20%).

**Adventure**
- Enemies are measured and sized to fit the fight scene, so big ones such as the Foe Reaper 4000 are no longer cut off, and every enemy stands on the ground.

**Settings**
- The Settings page is sorted into clear groups: Cat and display, Chat and sounds, Position, Help and info, and Progress.
- A new Text distance slider sets the space between the cat and its level, XP bar and counters. It works with the text above or below the cat, and the cat stays where it is.
- A new option, Share progress with the developer, is off by default. When it is on and the developer asks, your addon answers with five things: the addon version, your cat's class, cat level, zones cleared and TapCoins. Nothing else is sent. It only answers the developer, only while you are both online, and nothing from it prints in your chat. To hear the question your addon joins an addon channel, which the game still lists in its Chat Channels window.
- Help and info has the Welcome tour, Patch notes, Send feedback and a new Compendium button side by side.
- The minimap button says whether it will show or hide the icon.
- The two reset buttons sit on their own at the bottom, in red, so they are hard to click by accident. Both still ask first.
- Scroll the mouse wheel over a slider to move it one step at a time. The Ctrl and Shift mouse wheel tips for the cat are now in the Cat size and Opacity slider tooltips.

**Other fixes**
- Party cats: your own cat no longer shows up in the little row beside your cat.
- Messages, notices and achievement names use plainer wording, without exclamation marks.
- Up to 10 taps every second earn TapCoins, however fast you tap. Faster taps still count as taps and XP.

## 1.5.2

**Quieter loot**
- Nothing pops up over your cat anymore. Gear and cosmetics you find while playing (lucky finds from tapping, Adventure drops, quests, expeditions) go straight into your bags without a notice box.
- With the TapCatForever window open, gear you find shows as one slim line under the window that fades after a few seconds. Hover it to compare it with what your cat wears; click it to open the Gear tab.
- Opening a chest plays the reel and shows the loot box only for a blue (Rare) or better item. A common or green item from a chest gets the same slim line instead.
- Earning a chest, clearing a zone and an expedition coming back no longer show a note over the cat.

**Fixes**
- Hunters always have a bow: a hunter cat without a ranged weapon gets its bow, and the cat holds it.

## 1.5.1

**Each class has its own professions**
- Every class's cat now has its own Trade Gold, Renown, gathering spots, tool, helpers, upgrades, skills and materials, and its own Herbalism garden, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking. Before, all the cats on a character shared one pile of Trade Gold.
- Switch class and the Professions tab shows that cat's own business. The tab says whose gold you are looking at.
- The cats you aren't playing keep working: their foremen, trappers, scavengers, gardener, field medic, fishing buddy and sous-chef run online and offline (up to 24 hours), and what they earn goes to that cat. They do it quietly, without chat lines or loot pop-ups.
- Each cat earns its own milestone chests and sells its own business for its own Renown.
- A Well Fed buff helps the cat that ate the dish.
- Achievements count the Trade Gold of all your cats together, and the best Renown of one cat.

**Your progress moves over on its own**
- On your first login, everything your character had goes to the cat you are playing: its Trade Gold, Renown, unlocked professions, garden, bandages, fish, dishes and materials.
- If you had also built up another main profession by switching class (for example Mining on a Warrior while you play a Mage), those spots, its tool, upgrades, milestones, skills and materials go to the cat of that profession you played most. That cat starts with no Trade Gold or Renown of its own.
- If the cat you are playing has First Aid, it keeps the cloth that First Aid uses.
- Your other cats start fresh, at Renown 0. Each one learns Herbalism, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking with its own Renown, and its helpers earn without the Renown bonus of the cat you are playing.
- Your Trade Gold, spots, materials and items are not lost or doubled, and milestone chests you already got are not paid again.
- Speed boosts and spot prices now count only each cat's own spots. If you had spots in two professions, each cat's next spots cost less, but speed boosts that came from adding both together are worked out again from that cat's own spots.

**Adventure**
- Clearing a zone for the first time now pays a few hundred TapCoins on top of the chest: 200 for Elwynn Forest, 50 more for each zone after it (1,550 for Naxxramas). The first of your cats to clear a zone gets the full amount; another class clearing it later gets a quarter.

**Gear**
- Hovering a piece of gear shows the item your cat is wearing in that slot right beside it, like WoW's own comparison, along with the Power change.

**Loot**
- New chest: the Raider's Hoard. It always holds gear: Epic boss loot from the raids your cat has reached (the first raid if it has reached none). Raid bosses in Adventure drop one about every 3 hours of fighting, the first clear of a raid zone gives one, real WoW raids give one (once a week each), and the Molten Core, Blackwing Lair and Naxxramas expeditions sometimes bring one home. It is never sold in the Shop, and the Treasure Bag has a new row for it.
- Real Legendary gear can now drop, picked for your class: about 1 in 500 Heroic Caches and 1 in 100 Raider's Hoards hold one. Before, a Legendary roll gave a raid boss Epic.
- The Cat Treat Pouch, the smallest chest, keeps its small chance of a Rare (blue) piece: about 1 in 12 pouches. It now gives one at every level. Before, at levels with no blue gear close by, it quietly gave a green instead.
- Much more world gear for every class: about 750 white (Common) pieces with plain armor or damage, and about 1,600 Classic green items that drop with random bonuses such as "of the Monkey", "of the Bear" or "of the Owl" (about 4,900 versions in all).
- Small finds: trash enemies in Adventure now and then drop a white or green piece for your level. They favour slots your cat has empty, then upgrades, so a new cat fills most of its slots in its first hour or so. Finds slow down as your slots fill and as your cat finds more, so chests and bosses stay the main source of good loot.
- White gear is never in chests. Sell junk and the Gear page handle it like other gear.

**Professions help**
- Each profession now explains itself: the first time you open one, a short "How it works" guide shows how gathering, crafting real gear with stats or cosmetic-only items, upgrades and Renown work, and where crafted items go. The Help button next to the profession's name opens it again.

**Other fixes**
- The welcome tour now always reaches new players, even if something else went wrong while logging in.
- Prices and sources on item squares (Appearance tab and Compendium) are readable again: the text sat under the faded item picture.
- Send feedback is in Settings (and /tcf feedback) instead of the top of the window.
- Test mode: a Remove fake cats button, and fake party cats go away when you leave a group.

## 1.5.0

**Professions by class**
- Your cat's class now picks its main profession. Warriors and Paladins get Mining with Blacksmithing. Rogues, Druids, Hunters and Shamans get Skinning with Leatherworking. Mages, Priests and Warlocks get Tailoring.
- Each one is the same idle business with the same numbers under its own names. Mining has Veins, a Pickaxe and Foremen. Skinning has Hunting Grounds (real beasts from higher-level zones, from Barrens Plainstriders to Devilsaurs), a Skinning Knife and Trappers. Tailoring has Cloth Spots (Defias Hideout, Syndicate Camp, Bloodsail Camp and more), Shears and Scavengers.
- Hunting Grounds and Cloth Spots show the real creatures in 3D.
- Each class's profession keeps its own progress, and your hired helpers keep working, online and offline, even while your cat is a different class.
- Herbalism, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking still unlock later for everyone, at the same Renown costs.
- Existing characters keep their progress: on first login a cloth or leather class moves its Mining spots, tool level, upgrades, milestones and skill to its new profession, and its ore becomes cloth or leather of the same tier.

**Crafting real gear**
- Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring craft real Classic gear with stats, 45 recipes each from skill 1 to 300. Blacksmiths make plate, mail, weapons and shields, leatherworkers make leather and mail, tailors make cloth armor.
- Crafting skill rises the classic way: orange recipes always give a point, yellow often, green sometimes, grey never. Reaching 300 is a long-term goal of several weeks.
- Recipes you can't make yet are listed with the skill they need, so you can see what comes next.
- Every cosmetic recipe says "Cosmetic only (no stats)" in the recipe list, its tooltip and the Compendium. You can't craft a cosmetic you already own.
- Leatherworking and Tailoring each get 5 cosmetic recipes and their own craftable cat skins.
- Crafted gear goes into your current class's gear bag and its tooltip says which profession made it. It is never found as loot, and it sells for Trade Gold, not TapCoins.

**Play with friends**
- Friends in your party or raid who also have TapCatForever show up as small cats in a row beside yours. Hover one to see whose cat it is.
- Your taps count 20% more with one of them and 10% more for each one after that, up to +50% with a full party: more tap progress, cat XP and TapCoins from tapping.
- The addons find each other on their own, across realms, and reconnect after a reload or a fight.

**Fishing dock**
- The Fishing page is now a cartoon dock over a lake. Your cat sits on the dock with a fishing pole and the bobber floats on the water.
- Once you hire a Fishing Buddy, he sits beside your cat with his own pole, and wears a fishing hat once he levels up.

**New cat skins**
- 33 new cat skins, each with its own fur pattern in the cartoon style.
- In the Shop: Mittens, Tuxedo Cat, Cow Cat, Russian Blue, Dalmatian Cat, Tortoiseshell and Snowshoe (300-650 TapCoins), Bengal, Maine Coon and Strawberry Sweetheart (1,000-1,200), and Galaxy Cat (3,200).
- Rare Adventure boss drops (2-5% per kill): Ragefire Cinder Cat, Defias Bandit Cat, Stone Watcher Cat, Maraudon Crystal Cat, Scarlet Crusader Cat, Wind-Up Clockwork Cat, Onyxian Whelpling Cat, Qiraji Scarab Cat and Patchwork Kitty.
- Chest-only cats: Lucky Clover Cat and Rainbow Cat (Adventurer's Strongbox), Golden Treasure Cat and Diamond Cat (Heroic Cache).
- Quest cats (Quest-Giver Cat, Ready-to-Turn-In Cat), expedition cats (Emberheart Cat, Runebound Cat), real dungeon and raid cats (Party Animal, Raid Leader Cat) and craftable cats.

**Compendium**
- A new Compendium window (button at the top of the main window, or /tcf compendium) lists every skin, cosmetic, tap effect, effect and piece of gear, what you own, and how to get the rest.

**Loot and gear**
- Gear and cosmetics you find no longer open a big loot box. They go straight into your bags and a small notice appears by your cat: hover it to compare with what you wear, click it to open the Gear or Appearance tab, right-click to dismiss. Opening a chest still plays the reel.
- Every gear tooltip compares the item with what your cat is wearing, as a Power change.
- "Sell unequipped" is now "Sell junk": it sells gear that would lower your Power and extra copies you can't wear. Upgrades are kept, even ones you need a higher level for.
- Starter weapons fit the class: a bow for hunters, a dagger for rogues, a mace for paladins, priests and shamans, a sword for warriors and a staff for mages, warlocks and druids.

**Other**
- A short "What's new" note appears at the top of the window after a big update, and the full patch notes are in Settings (or /tcf news).
- New Send feedback window (button at the top of the main window, in Settings, or /tcf feedback).
- Enemies in Adventure are no longer cut off at the edges of their boxes.
- Cloth now sells for the same as ore and leather.

## 1.4.0

**More enemies in Adventure**
- Every zone, dungeon and raid now has its real Classic creatures: up to 16 different enemies per zone (335 in all, up from 56), with real models taken from a full Classic creature database. Molten Core goes from 2 creature types to 13, Naxxramas from 3 to 15, Elwynn Forest from 3 to 13.
- More bosses now use their own real models instead of stand-ins.
- A pack no longer has the same creature twice when the zone has enough variety.
- Enemies play their death animation and fade out (including the last one in a pack and bosses), and killing blows show their spell effect.
- Damage and healing numbers float above enemies instead of behind them, and the ability buttons flash when your cat uses an ability.

**Adventure fixes and balance**
- Fixed a bug that kept Divine Shield, Ice Block, Recklessness and other buffs up almost all the time and reset every cooldown at the start of each fight. Abilities now last and recharge as their tooltips say, so some raid bosses are harder.
- Adventure chests are now based on time spent fighting: about one Cat Treat Pouch per 15-25 minutes in zones and dungeons, and one Adventurer's Strongbox per 30 minutes in raids, dropped by a boss. Trash no longer drops pouches.
- Auto-advance off now really means "keep farming this stage": a lost fight retries the same stage. After 3 losses in a row your cat drops back to an easier pack. With Auto-advance on, a cat that loses at the start of a zone falls back to the previous zone.
- Bandages in fights work again after their cooldown, instead of staying locked.

**Gear and chests**
- A chest's Rare or Epic roll no longer quietly turns into a green item; the chest looks a little wider for your level before dropping a quality.
- Chests keep giving proper gear after your cat passes level 60.
- Brand-new cats no longer get very strong endgame gear: items with no level requirement now need roughly their item level minus 5. Every class starts with a fair level-10 weapon.
- Boss drops are always gear your class can wear. Blizzard's leftover test items are gone from loot.
- Equipping a one-hander while wearing a two-hander now replaces the two-hander. With both ring or trinket slots full, Equip replaces the weaker one. The "+power" number is now correct in every case, and you can equip a second copy of a ring, trinket or one-hander.
- Selling: tooltips say exactly what Sell does, you're asked before selling your only copy of a rare or epic, and "Sell unequipped" no longer stutters with a big bag.
- Hovering gear previews it on your cat even when that slot has a cosmetic.
- The Shop shows the real chest odds (90% gear, 10% cosmetic).

**Cartoon cat**
- Hunter bows, guns, crossbows and thrown weapons now show in your cat's paw, and capes show on its back in their own colours.
- Gear that shares another slot's icon now draws in the right place (no more belts on helms or daggers from necklaces).
- Floating effects bob smoothly and stay centred.
- Scrolling over the cat zooms the camera again (hold Ctrl or Shift to resize or fade the cat).
- The cat no longer leaves an invisible strip that blocked clicks, and it stays exactly where you put it after this update.

**Professions**
- Pickaxe swings on higher veins now pay a fair share of that vein's output instead of beating your whole business.
- Mr. Pinchy's gold wish now gives about 10 minutes of what your mines make. Your fishing buddy finds treasure much less often than you do when you fish yourself, and lists offline finds in one line.
- Cooking: ingredients taken from the pot before you cook now empty the pot (no more free dishes), Burnt Mess rarely raises your skill, and there's a new recipe to discover that uses Raptor Flesh.
- First Aid: the Field Medic makes the best bandage you have cloth for, and stalled queues say what cloth they need.
- Herbalism: clicking a locked plot no longer buys a different plot.

**Expeditions, quests and dungeons**
- Short expeditions are now mainly for XP and TapCoins; chests come mostly from the long ones. Tooltips show the real chest odds.
- You're told when a party gets back (on the cat, and in chat if chat messages are on), even if it came back while you were offline.
- Quests track progress correctly across your characters and can't show negative numbers. Boss quests take about as long as the others.
- Real dungeon and raid chests: re-entering after stepping out or a wipe continues the same run. Each dungeon gives a chest once a day, each raid once a week, and up to 2 dungeon chests a day across your characters. You're told when a run gives no chest.

**Account and characters**
- Achievement rewards (cosmetics and cat skins such as Mr. Bigglesworth) now appear on all your characters, and you keep them after "Reset this character".
- "Citizen of Azeroth" counts heritage pieces from all your characters, and "Completionist" can reach 100% again.
- Each character has its own Daily Wares.
- Playing past midnight gives you the new day's treat and keeps your streak.
- Slash commands and replies to your own clicks always show in chat, even with "Chat messages" turned off; that setting now only mutes passive notices.

**Interface**
- The Treasure Bag is wider so chest names aren't hidden, the loot box switches to a new reel's item instead of hiding it, and "Open all" no longer freezes the game with a big pile of chests.
- Achievements show their cat-skin rewards, and the Appearance and Shop pages no longer overlap their buttons.

- **Real WoW gear**: 8,661 real Classic items with their real stats (Strength, Stamina, Armor, weapon DPS, crit, spell power...), drop sources and drop chances. 17 gear slots per class (Head to Ranged), Classic armor and weapon rules per class, and the cat wears its real gear (cosmetics still override the look).

- New "Text size" setting, separate from cat size, so a tiny cat can keep readable text.
- New quests and achievements for gear, expeditions and crafting.

- **Adventure**: a new idle game tab. Your cat fights through 12 tiers of classic zones, dungeons and raids (Elwynn Forest to Naxxramas, then endless Heroic tiers), using the gear equipped on the current class. Every key press lands a bonus paw strike. Bosses drop cosmetics.
- **Quests**: 3 daily quests (TapCoins + a Cat Treat Pouch each) and a weekly quest (TapCoins + an Adventurer's Strongbox).
- **Real dungeon and raid rewards**: boss kills pay TapCoins; finishing a dungeon gives an Adventurer's Strongbox, a raid gives a Heroic Cache.
- **Treasure Bag** for unopened chests, and a **loot reveal box** that shows what you got.
- **Per-class levels**: each class levels up on its own, starting at 1.
- **Gear slots** around the cat in the window: click a slot to choose its item, right-click to empty it.
- A / left arrow and left mouse tap the left paw; D / right arrow and right mouse tap the right paw; other keys alternate.
- Weapons, shields and other held items now sit in front of the paws.
- The cat in the window no longer taps on its own.
- TapCoins are scarcer: 1 per 50 key presses; quests and Adventure are the main income. Daily wares show 3 items.
