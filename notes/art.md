# Art: animated cosmetics, shoulders and the racial slot

## Animated cosmetics
- Cosmetics can now move on your cat: flickering flames, lava that flows, glows that breathe, stars drifting inside a helm or through the fur, rising embers, falling snow and leaves, bubbles, sparkles and ghostly wisps.
- Effects only play on the cartoon cat and stop while the cat is hidden. Small cats (the party row) show only the light parts.

## Effects on the expensive skins
- Every Epic and Legendary cat skin now has its own animated effect.
- Diamond Cat sparkles with glints and a shine that sweeps across its fur.
- Galaxy Cat has stars and soft nebula clouds drifting inside its fur.
- Spectral Tiger glows and trails ghostly wisps.
- Golden Treasure Cat shimmers with a gold shine and glints.
- Fire cats (Primal Flamesaber, Emberheart, Onyxian Whelpling, Ragefire Cinder, Vermillion Lynx) give off embers, and Primal Flamesaber has fire moving in its fur.
- Frost cats (Winterspring Frostsaber, Mr. Bigglesworth) have falling snow and frost glints.
- Shadow cats (Shadow Dusk Dreamsaber, Swift Zulian Panther, Skeletal Cat) trail wisps; Rainbow Cat's markings shift colour; Runebound Cat's runes pulse.
- Legendary cosmetic pieces shine: a light sweeps across them now and then. Frozen Throne Crown, Crown of the Tapmaster, Wings of the Eternal Tapper, Felcloth Wings and Black Lotus have their own sparkles, snow or embers.
- The Spectral and Starlight furs glow and twinkle.

## Molten Core set
- A new animated fire set, cosmetic only. Each piece always drops from one Molten Core boss in Adventure until you own it:
  - Crown of the Firelord (crown with flickering flames): Ragnaros
  - Golemagg's Molten Pauldrons (shoulders): Golemagg the Incinerator
  - Lava-Heart Breastplate (lava flows through its seams, its heart glows): Garr
  - Magmadar's Cinder Drape (cape with burning edges): Magmadar
  - Sulfuron Firehammer (flaming hammer): Lucifron
  - Baron Geddon's Ember Aura (a heat glow and rising embers): Baron Geddon
- The Compendium and tooltips show the set's progress, such as "Molten Core set (2/6)", and which boss drops each piece.

## Shoulders
- The cat has a new Shoulders slot in the Appearance tab.
- Real shoulder gear now shows on your cat as pauldrons on both shoulders, in the colours of the item: plate, mail, leather and cloth each have their own shape.
- Every real shoulder look in the game has its own cartoon drawing, made from the item's icon (43 in all): gold domes over mail, tall red fins, quilted leather, shaggy mantles with orbs, the tauren skull, the glowing Netherwind runes, Ten Storms lightning and more.
- Pauldrons sit over capes and chest pieces and under hoods and big helms, and your paws stay in front of them.
- Party members see your shoulders too.

## Racial accessory has its own slot
- The racial heritage accessory (tusks, horns, ears, beard, goggles, feathers...) now has its own slot, so you can wear it together with sunglasses, a monocle, a bow tie or any other accessory.
- Face accessories sit on top of it (shades over tusks), neck accessories under it (a dwarf beard over a bow tie).
- "Show racial accessory" in Settings still hides or shows it.
- If you wore it in the accessory slot before, it moves to the new slot on its own.
- Party members on this version see both; older versions see the accessory only.
