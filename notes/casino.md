# The TapCat Casino

## Where to find it
- A new Casino tab sits between Leaderboard and Settings in the TapCatForever window.
- The Casino fills the whole window under the tabs. Pick Slots, Blackjack or Death roll with the buttons at the top.
- The line at the top shows your bet limits, and your stats for the game you are playing (hover it for every game).

## Bets
- A bet is at least 10 TapCoins and at most a quarter of your TapCoins, up to 500.
- You need at least 40 TapCoins to play.
- Use the - and + buttons or the quick 10, 50, 100 and Max buttons to choose your bet. Your bet is kept for next time.
- Every game keeps a small house edge, so over time the Casino takes a few TapCoins out of the game. It is for fun, not for getting rich. There are no free spins.
- Casino bets and wins don't count toward the TapCoins earned and spent achievements.

## Lucky Paws slots
- A cartoon cat slot machine with three reels. Pull the lever or press Spin.
- The reels spin, slow down and stop from left to right. Click the reels to stop them at once.
- Only the middle line counts. Three of a kind wins, and every cat on the line pays something.
- Pays (times your bet, your bet included): three cats 100, three gems 40, three bells 25, three coins 15, three fish 10, three yarn balls 6, three paws 3, two cats 5, one cat 2.
- About 3 spins in 10 win something. Over many spins the machine pays back about 94% of what is bet.
- Hover the "How it works" text for the chance of every line.
- A big win (10 times your bet or more) gets a coin shower, flashing lights and a cheer from your cat, inside the window only.

## Blackjack
- Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Picture cards count 10, aces 1 or 11.
- A win pays 1 to 1. A blackjack (an ace and a 10 card as your first two cards) pays 3 to 2. A tie is a push: you get your bet back.
- The dealer draws to 17 and stands on every 17, soft 17 too. The dealer checks for blackjack first.
- Double: double your bet on any first two cards and take exactly one more card.
- Split: split a pair into two hands once. Split aces get one card each.
- No insurance. Six decks, shuffled again when a quarter of the shoe is left.
- With perfect play the house keeps about half a percent.
- Closing the window doesn't lose your hand: it waits for you, even after a reload. A win is always paid, even if you close the window while the cards are still being dealt.

## Achievements
- Natural: get a blackjack (25 TapCoins).
- Three Cats in a Row: line up three cats on the slots (100 TapCoins).
